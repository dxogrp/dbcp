# Citing

If you find DBCP useful in your research, please consider citing the following
paper.

```bibtex
@article{zhu2025dbcp,
  title={Disciplined Biconvex Programming},
  author={Zhu, H. and Boedecker, J.},
  journal={arXiv Preprint arXiv:2511.01813},
  year={2025},
}
```

Catalog links and additional materials are available on
[the paper page](https://haozhu10015.github.io/papers/dbcp.html).
